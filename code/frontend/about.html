About chargEV Patiala

Building the definitive full-stack smart charging ecosystem for electric vehicles across Patiala and surrounding regions.

Our Mission

chargEV is engineered to solve range anxiety and streamline station discovery across major Patiala hubs including Kalyan, Focal Point, Urban Estate Phase III, Rajpura Road, and Muradpur. By integrating real-time telemetry, QR code slot reservations, and robust Node.js/MongoDB persistence, we provide a seamless experience for EV owners.

Local Network

Verified high-speed DC fast chargers and AC Type 2 ports across Patiala.

QR Slot Booking

Lock in time windows instantly and unlock charging terminals via secure QR passes.

Live Telemetry

Monitor SoC percentages, kilowatt rates, and accrued session costs in real time.
